I recently forgot how to feel. maybe it was the days that kept on passing by. maybe it was the sun setting around 4:30 in the afternoon as it got cold outside. I closed my windows and our conversations stopped. she would be smiling and laughing from afar but somehow she lost that glint in her eyes. you locked me in without even knowing and behind those closed doors I’d call out your name. talk to me it’s always been just you and me. in that moment, the train stopped in the midst of its tracks, she stopped to catch a breath and her whole world fell apart. an avalanche of burning sensations swept over, bringing back everything she ever cared for. without a key that should have been what opened the lock. and that scared the shit out of her. where were you when I needed you. I’m lost and these endless flight of stairs aren’t showing me a way out. I’ve lost my sense of direction and now I’m swimming in an ocean full of memories. don’t forget. she’ll remember.
